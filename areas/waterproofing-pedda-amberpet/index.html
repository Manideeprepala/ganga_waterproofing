Waterproofing for Pedda Amberpet's homes and buildings

Pedda Amberpet sits on the Vijayawada Highway (NH 65) at the Outer Ring Road junction on Hyderabad's eastern edge, with Kuntloor, Pasumamula and Tatti Annaram part of the same municipality. The area has grown quickly with new independent houses, gated layouts and apartment blocks, and many of these buildings face their first few monsoons without proper waterproofing.

The problems we see most often here are terrace leaks into top-floor rooms, seepage on outer walls exposed to heavy rain, bathroom leakage into the rooms next door, and leaking sumps. Each needs a different treatment, so every job starts with a free site inspection to find where the water is really coming from.

Terrace waterproofing for new and older houses

Flat RCC terraces take the full force of summer heat and monsoon rain. For terraces in good condition, a fibreglass mesh reinforced coating gives a seamless waterproof layer that bridges hairline cracks, and its white finish keeps the rooms below cooler. Where the old flooring is cracked or hollow, we remove it, waterproof the slab and lay new flooring with the right slope. Mesh coating comes with a 5-year written warranty, and terrace flooring with up to 10 years.
