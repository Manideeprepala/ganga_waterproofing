Waterproofing for Secunderabad's homes and buildings

Secunderabad has some of the oldest buildings in Hyderabad alongside newer apartment blocks. Older houses and institutional buildings often have large flat roofs, porticos, chajjas and thick walls that have been through decades of monsoons. Over time, cracks in the roof slab and open joints at the edges let rain seep in, and it shows up as damp ceilings, peeling plaster and stains on the walls.

In apartments around Marredpally, Bowenpally and Trimulgherry, the most common problems are bathroom seepage into the flat below, terrace leakage on the top floor and damp exterior walls. Each needs a different treatment, so every job starts with a site inspection to trace the water to its real source.

Recent work: portico waterproofing at Military Hospital, Secunderabad

Our team recently carried out portico waterproofing at Military Hospital, Secunderabad. A portico slab takes direct sun and rain every day, and water collecting on top slowly finds its way through cracks into the ceiling below. We cleaned the slab, treated the cracks and joints, and applied a waterproof coating so the portico stays dry through the rains.

Terrace waterproofing in Secunderabad

We also take up terrace waterproofing for homes and buildings across Secunderabad. The terrace is cleaned, cracks and joints are sealed, and a waterproof coating is applied in layers so the rooms below stay dry.
