Waterproofing services we offer in Ramanthapur

  • Terrace waterproofingCrack and joint treatment followed by fibre-mesh, PU or membrane systems for roof leaks and top-floor ceiling damp.
  • Bathroom waterproofingStops seepage into adjoining rooms and the flat below, with or without breaking tiles depending on the cause.
  • Exterior wall waterproofingCrack filling, parapet and chajja sealing, and water-repellent coatings for damp walls.
  • Water tank & sump waterproofingLeak repair for overhead RCC tanks and underground sumps.
  • Leak detection & injection groutingWe trace hidden leaks and seal cracks from inside with PU or epoxy injection.
  • Epoxy groutingStain-proof, waterproof tile joints for bathrooms, kitchens and floors.
