Waterproofing for Panjagutta's homes and buildings

Panjagutta (also spelt Punjagutta) is a busy junction in central Hyderabad, between Ameerpet, Somajiguda, Khairatabad and Banjara Hills, and has a station on the Metro Red Line. It is a mix of older independent houses, apartment blocks, shops and offices, and many of the older buildings now have terraces and bathrooms whose original waterproofing has worn out.

The problems we see most often here are leaking terraces with cracked or hollow old flooring, damp ceilings in top-floor rooms, bathroom leakage into the rooms next door, and seepage on outer walls after heavy rain. Each needs a different treatment, so every job starts with a free site inspection to find where the water is really coming from.

Terrace waterproofing for older buildings

Flat RCC terraces take the full force of summer heat and monsoon rain. Where the old flooring is cracked or hollow, we remove it, waterproof the slab and lay new flooring with the right slope. For terraces still in good condition, a fibreglass mesh reinforced coating gives a seamless waterproof layer that bridges hairline cracks, and its white finish keeps the rooms below cooler. Terrace flooring comes with up to 10 years written warranty, and mesh coating with 5 years.
