Waterproofing for Dilsukhnagar's homes and buildings

Dilsukhnagar is one of the busiest parts of east Hyderabad, with older independent houses, apartment blocks and shops built close together along the main roads. Many of these buildings have been through years of heavy monsoons, and water usually finds its way in through the same weak spots: gaps around window frames, cracks in outer walls, open joints on flat terraces and leaking bathroom floors.

In apartments around Kothapet, Chaitanyapuri and Saroornagar, the most common complaints are damp patches under windows, seepage on the outer walls after rain and bathroom leakage into the flat below. Each needs a different treatment, so every job starts with a site inspection to find where the water is really coming from.
