What we do

Waterproofing for every surface water finds

Every structure leaks differently. We match the system — membrane, coating, grouting or coba — to how your building actually behaves.

Terrace & roof waterproofing

The single biggest source of seepage in Hyderabad homes. We treat cracks and joints first, then lay a fibre-mesh reinforced coating that flexes with the slab instead of cracking with it.

Bathroom waterproofing

A damp patch on the ceiling below or the wall behind the shower usually traces back to one failed joint or pipe sleeve. We find it, seal the sunken slab and corners, and finish with epoxy grout so water stays on the tile.

Sunken slabEpoxyPre-tile treatment
Learn more

Balcony waterproofing

Balconies fail at the slope and the door threshold. We re-profile the fall, seal the parapet junction and finish with a coating that survives direct sun and monsoon both.

Slope correctionJoint sealingUV-stable
Learn more

Exterior & side walls

Driven rain on a north wall soaks through porous plaster within two seasons. We fill hairline cracks, apply a breathable water-repellent system and stop the damp at the outside face.

Crack fillingElastomericBreathable

Factory roofs, warehouse floors and plant rooms, planned around your shift timings. Liquid membrane, PU, bituminous or EPDM — specified for load, chemical exposure and traffic.

Questions

Things people ask us first

It's priced per square foot and depends on the area, its current condition and the system needed. A terrace with sound concrete costs far less than one with active cracks. That's why the inspection is free — you get a firm, itemised number before committing to anything.

A single bathroom is usually two to three days. An average terrace runs three to five days depending on size and the number of coats. Industrial roofs are scheduled around your operations. Curing time between coats is non-negotiable — rushing it is the most common reason waterproofing fails.

Almost never. Terrace and exterior work happens entirely outside your living space. For bathroom work you'll lose the use of that one bathroom for a few days. We clear the site each evening so you're not living around the job.

Every job is handed over with a written warranty covering workmanship and the applied system. The term depends on the material specified and is stated clearly on your quote before work begins — so you know what you're covered for, and for how long.

We can carry out emergency stop-gap treatment and injection grouting in wet conditions to control an active leak. Full coating systems need a dry, cured surface — we'll tell you honestly which of the two your situation calls for rather than applying a coating that won't bond.

We work across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, and travel to sites on the outskirts for larger commercial and industrial projects. Our base is in Ramanthapur. Call us with your location and we'll confirm straight away.
