Should You Remove Old Terrace Flooring Before Waterproofing?

Many terraces in Hyderabad have tiles or screed that look fine from above but keep leaking into the ceiling below. Sometimes the old flooring can stay. Sometimes it has to come off first. Here is how to tell, and what we did on a recent terrace job in Turkayamjal.

By Ganga Waterproofing Services · 18 October 2026

Why coating over damaged flooring often fails

A waterproof coating can only be as good as the surface it sits on. When terrace tiles or screed have separated from the slab, water gets into the gap underneath through cracked joints and edges. It then travels under the flooring, sometimes several feet, before it finds a crack in the slab and drips into the room below. Painting a coating over the top does not stop the water that is already moving underneath, and the coating soon cracks as the loose flooring shifts.

Signs the old flooring needs to come off

  • Hollow sound when you tap the tiles or screed with a coin or tool handle.
  • Loose or rocking tiles, or tiles that have lifted at the edges.
  • Cracks that keep coming back in the same place after every repair.
  • Water seeping out of joints long after the rain has stopped, a sign water is trapped underneath.
  • Puddles that stay for hours because the flooring has sunk or lost its slope.
  • Leaks in the ceiling far away from any visible crack on the terrace.

If none of these apply and the flooring is firmly bonded, it can often stay. The joints and edges are sealed and a suitable system is applied over it, which saves time and cost.

What we do when the flooring has to be removed

  1. Inspect and mark. We check the terrace and the ceiling below, tap-test the flooring and mark the damaged areas.
  2. Chip off the damaged flooring. Loose tiles, hollow screed and failed old coatings are removed down to a sound base.
  3. Clean the slab. Dust and debris are cleared so new materials bond properly.
  4. Open and fill the cracks. Every crack in the slab is opened up, cleaned and filled, with extra care at parapet corners, pipe openings and tank bases.
  5. Apply the waterproofing system in layers, chosen after seeing the condition of the slab.
  6. Test before handover. A water test confirms the terrace is holding before the job is closed.

A real job: terrace waterproofing in Turkayamjal

On a recent terrace waterproofing job in Turkayamjal, Hyderabad, the old flooring was not in a condition to coat over, so our team started by chipping it off down to the slab.

With the slab exposed, the cracks underneath could be treated properly. The next day the team moved on to crack filling, so the waterproofing layers would sit on a sound, sealed surface.

Should you remove the flooring on your terrace?

It depends on what is under it, and that is why every job starts with an inspection. Removing good flooring wastes money; keeping bad flooring wastes the waterproofing.
