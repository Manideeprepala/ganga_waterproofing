Why bathrooms leak into the flat below

In most Hyderabad apartments the bathroom floor sits in a sunken slab, a recessed area filled with brickbats or rubble that hides the drain pipes. When the waterproofing under the tiles fails, or a pipe joint or floor trap leaks, water collects in that filling and slowly seeps into the ceiling of the flat below or the wall of the next room.

The usual quick fix of re-grouting the tiles rarely lasts, because the water is already below them. Our bathroom waterproofing in Hyderabad first identifies whether the problem is tile joints, a pipe, the floor trap or the slab itself. Where the leak is limited to the joints, we can often fix it without breaking tiles using epoxy grouting. Where the slab is saturated, we open it up and waterproof it properly.

Signs of bathroom leakage

  • Damp ceiling in the flat belowThe most common complaint, usually directly under the shower or floor trap.
  • Wet skirting on the adjoining wallPaint peeling or salt deposits on the wall shared with a bedroom or hall.
  • Blackened or missing groutTile joints that stay dark, crumble or wash out let water straight under the tiles.
  • Hollow-sounding tilesTiles that sound hollow when tapped often have water trapped beneath them.
  • Water around the floor trap or WCLeaks at the Nahani trap, WC outlet or pipe sleeves where they pass through the slab.
  • Persistent bad smellStagnant water in the sunken slab filling can cause a lingering odour.

Our bathroom waterproofing process

  1. Leak diagnosis

    We check tile joints, pipe sleeves, the floor trap, WC outlet and wall junctions, and run water tests to isolate the source.

  2. Choose the right repair

    Joint-only leaks can be fixed by epoxy grouting without breaking tiles. A saturated sunken slab needs to be opened and treated.

  3. Sunken slab & pipe treatment

    Pipe sleeves and outlets are sealed, and the slab and walls get a cementitious or polymer waterproof coating with reinforced corners.

  4. Pond test

    The waterproofed area is flooded and left standing to confirm there is no seepage before any tiling.

  5. Tiling & epoxy grout finish

    Tiles are relaid where needed and finished with epoxy grout, which does not absorb water or stain.

Bathroom waterproofing methods

Epoxy grouting (no tile breaking)

Old cement grout is raked out and replaced with waterproof epoxy grout. Suitable when the tiles and slab are still sound.

Cementitious / polymer coating

Two-component flexible coating applied on the sunken slab and up the walls before tiling.

Pipe sleeve & floor trap sealing

Joints where pipes pass through the slab are sealed with non-shrink grout and sealant, the most common leak point.

Pre-tile treatment for new bathrooms

Building or renovating? We waterproof the slab before the tiles go down, which is the cheapest time to get it right.
