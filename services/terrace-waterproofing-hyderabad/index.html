Why terraces in Hyderabad start leaking

The terrace is the single biggest source of seepage in Hyderabad homes. Summer surface temperatures regularly climb past 45°C, then the monsoon arrives with sudden, heavy downpours. That daily cycle of heating and cooling opens hairline cracks in the RCC slab and the old plaster screed, and every crack becomes a path for rainwater into the ceiling below.

Most repeat leaks happen because a coating is painted over a slab that was never properly prepared: loose screed, open joints and blocked outlets are simply covered up. Our terrace waterproofing in Hyderabad starts with fixing the surface, the slope and the drainage, and only then applies the waterproofing layer. If the water is travelling from somewhere unexpected, we trace the leak to its source before quoting.

Signs your roof needs waterproofing

  • Damp patches on the top-floor ceilingStains that grow darker after rain, often near corners and beams.
  • Peeling paint and plasterPaint bubbling or flaking on the ceiling or the upper part of walls.
  • Cracks on the terrace surfaceHairline or map cracks in the screed, especially around parapets and water tanks.
  • Water pooling after rainPuddles that stay for hours mean the slope or outlets need correcting.
  • Rusty stains or exposed steelA sign water has already reached the reinforcement, which needs urgent attention.
  • Musty smell or fungusPersistent dampness in top-floor rooms, wardrobes and false ceilings.

Our terrace waterproofing process

  1. Free inspection & diagnosis

    We examine the terrace, the ceiling below, outlets, parapets and tank bases to find exactly where water is getting in.

  2. Surface preparation

    Loose screed is removed, the surface is cleaned, and the slope is corrected where water ponds.

  3. Crack & joint treatment

    Cracks are opened and filled with polymer-modified mortar or sealant; parapet corners get a coved fillet so the corner can't split.

  4. Waterproofing system

    A fibre-mesh reinforced elastomeric coating, APP membrane or PU coating, chosen for your slab, budget and how the terrace is used.

  5. Protective top coat & testing

    A UV-stable, heat-reflective finish, followed by a water test and handover with a written warranty.

Terrace waterproofing systems we use

Fibre-mesh elastomeric coating

Flexible liquid coating reinforced with glass-fibre mesh. Stretches over hairline cracks. Ideal for most residential terraces.

APP / SBS bituminous membrane

Torch-applied sheet membrane for large flat roofs and slabs that will be covered with tiles or screed.

PU (polyurethane) coating

Seamless, highly elastic and UV-resistant. Suits terraces with foot traffic and complex shapes.

Brick bat coba

The traditional lime-and-brickbat layer with a waterproof compound, used on new construction for slope and insulation.

Heat-reflective cool-roof coat

White reflective top layer that lowers slab temperature, protects the membrane and keeps top floors cooler in summer.

Terrace Waterproofing: frequently asked questions
