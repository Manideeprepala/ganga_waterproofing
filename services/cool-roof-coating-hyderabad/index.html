Why top floors get so hot in Hyderabad summers

In April and May, Hyderabad afternoons regularly go past 40°C, and a bare concrete terrace soaks up the sun all day. The slab stores that heat and keeps releasing it into the top-floor rooms well into the night, which is why the top floor feels like an oven and the fan only blows hot air. A grey or dark terrace absorbs most of the sunlight that falls on it.

A cool roof coating changes that. It is a white, heat-reflective layer on the terrace that bounces much of the sunlight back instead of absorbing it, so the slab heats up less and the rooms below stay cooler. Telangana's Cool Roof Policy 2023–28 encourages cool roofs on buildings across the state to reduce heat stress.

Our cool roof coating: cooling and waterproofing in one

Many people simply paint the terrace white. That reflects some heat for a few months, but ordinary paint is thin, wears off and does nothing to stop leaks. We use a white fibreglass mesh reinforced coating, a proper waterproofing system with a heat-reflective finish. The glass-fibre mesh bridges hairline cracks, the coating forms a seamless layer with no joints, and the white top coat reflects the summer sun. You get a cooler home and a leak-free terrace from one job, with a 5-year written warranty.

Our cool roof coating process

  1. Free inspection

    We check the terrace for cracks, hollow areas, slope and drain outlets, and tell you honestly whether coating is enough or the old flooring needs replacing first.

  2. Cleaning & preparation

    The terrace is washed, loose material is removed and the surface is left to dry so the coating bonds properly.

  3. Crack & joint treatment

    Cracks are filled and the corners where the terrace meets the parapet walls are rounded off so they do not split.

  4. Fibreglass mesh coating

    A base coat is applied, the glass-fibre mesh is pressed into it, and further coats build up a strong, flexible waterproof layer.

  5. White reflective finish & handover

    The final white heat-reflective coat is applied and cured, and you get a written warranty.

Cool roof coating vs ordinary white paint

White paint or lime wash

Reflects some heat at first, but it is thin, gets dirty and washes off within a season or two. It does not seal cracks or stop leaks, and comes with no warranty.

Our cool roof mesh coating

A thick, flexible, fibre-reinforced coating that reflects the sun and waterproofs the terrace at the same time. It bridges hairline cracks and comes with a 5-year written warranty.

Best time to apply: before the summer

The coating needs a dry surface, so the dry months from December to March are the best time to get it done.
